Where it stands

Young, working, and deliberately not for sale.

Honesty is part of the design, so here are the plain limits.

  • It is a working system, and a young one. Declared machines boot and judge themselves in an emulator today; no customer yet runs a critical production workload on it. The first iteration of the Cousbox restaurant platform is delivered and its box is the first Harobanda target; the Diko engagement is won and is in its study phase.
  • The kernel underneath is borrowed on purpose. It is the same long-term-support Linux that Ubuntu, Red Hat and the major distributions ship — its source fixed by a fingerprint, so any change to it would show, and built by your own compiler — and a board's firmware is the supplier's, fixed by fingerprint too. Borrowed is not the same as withdrawable; that distinction is the whole point.
  • Single-binary deployment is the goal, not yet the whole reality. Declaring, judging and emulating a machine need only the harb binary; producing a flashable hosted image still calls a standard kernel build under the hood today. Folding that into the one binary is a committed direction.
  • The application layer is further along than the machine. HaroServ and HaroScript are at version 0.9, ready to use and not finished, and MicroHaro is underway; today programs run through the engine's Luau and Ring surfaces, with Python, JavaScript and PHP as the surfaces broaden. The Softanza foundation and its intelligence layer are in active development.
  • The agent guarantees are the floor's, and they are real. The walls around each program, the per-program budgets, a boundary of routes declared in the file, the signed record of every boot and the judged trial exist today; pointing an autonomous agent at them, end to end, is the frontier.
  • The enterprise answers are at mixed maturity. The security posture is largely a property of the design and holds today (what it rests on, and what is not built); fleet-scale rollout, standard sign-in and integration connectors are partly built and partly a committed direction.
  • It will never be a product you buy from a vendor — because a vendor inside the system is the one thing it refuses. That is not a gap to close. It is the design.

Where it comes from

None of this came from a lab.

It came from real work, in Niamey and in France.

Sonibanka bank · Niamey
Demands provable changes, for the new version of our Organizium software.
ESPA-MTa higher-education school · Niamey
Teaches its students to design and govern their own AI. Our work is the foundation of its AI & Computational Thinking course.
DIKOa national NGO · Niamey
Keeps its sensitive data in-house: the host server for its Diko AI agent.
Cousboxa restaurant · Lyon, France
Needed a box server for RestoLean, our platform for neighbourhood commerce: customers order from their phone, with no account, and the order reaches the kitchen. On its first trial, on 15 August 2026, a fridge tripped the breaker and the router rebooted: the server's address changed, and two hours of observations were lost.

Not one of them runs on Harobanda yet. They are where it comes from.
