How these pages are written

Facts first, then the position, then what is not built.

  • The facts are somebody else's, and named. A position starts from a source a reader can go and read; the facts are condensed, never quoted at length, and nothing of the year is asserted on this site's own authority.
  • The position is said affirmatively. What the floor does against the challenge is said first and plainly; a limit is said as what the floor gives, never as a denial of what a reader was asking.
  • What is built is tagged. Every row that says the floor does something carries the same tag the rest of the site uses, and “emulated” means exactly that: nobody has booted it in a room.
  • What should be added is analysed, not promised. A missing declaration is named with what it buys and what it costs, and stays tagged as not built until the day a boot shows it.
