Resilience

A cloud that survives the loss of a room.

A hall struck, a link cut, a supplier out of reach: the threats of 2026 are physical, and the answer is in how many rooms a solution runs in and what each room's file says. Ten things a designer can already write into a fleet file and the court reads, two declarations the file cannot make yet, and what is not claimed. The position that asked for this page is in the blog.

Today

Ten things a designer can write, and the court reads.

Where a box sitsA member of a fleet is one machine on one link, with the hardware address the box answers to. Every member's place is a fact the court reads, and one the fleet's roll shows.built · emulated
Where the data staysA member says where it stands, and the fleet says where the data stays. A box that keeps a disk anywhere else is refused before anything boots; a till that keeps none can stand anywhere. The place is the operator's word and is never observed: whoever enrols a box vouches for where it is.built · judged by the court
A copyOne member gives the files of a program's own directory, another keeps them, over a link the fleet names. The keeper holds every file to a list the giver signed, keeps the last complete copy or none, and anyone holding the fleet file can check it with no secret. The giver follows no link and gives only the files the program owns, so the machine's own key cannot ride along; and a copy kept in another country than the data stays in is refused by the rule that already holds every disk. What crosses the link is signed and not encrypted: a change on the way is caught, and a host sitting on the link is not stopped from reading it.built · emulated
The roll callWho has written a record since. One command reads every member's record against the fleet file's keys and its time authority's statements, and against the roll before it: whose each record is, how far it goes, who has written since, who has nothing new, whose record went backwards, and which one nobody brought, with what the last roll held of it. "Since" is the last roll's last entry and not a clock, so a rebuilt card is not mistaken for the old one, a member nobody could bring is not forgotten, and a record that goes backwards stays flagged until a person accepts it. A machine that stays up writes nothing, so the roll says "nothing new" and does not judge it. The records are brought to it; nothing fetches them yet.built · emulated
The way betweenOne box says it is the way between two links, and the fleet says it was meant. A forwarder no route goes through is a door nobody agreed to, and is refused.built · emulated
What a box reachesA machine knows no way to anywhere it was not told. A box in a room can be told nothing but its own link, and the boot line says so after reading the routes back.built · emulated
Who is the doorOne member ends TLS for the devices of a network, by the name it has there and under a root the fleet names by its fingerprint. A device trusts the root it was given and nothing else, and the fingerprint its question prints is the one the file declares.built · emulated
Who dates a recordOne member is the fleet's time authority, and a record is ordered and undated until it signs. Anyone holding the fleet file can check a record's time with nothing else.built · emulated
Who is outA retired key is trusted through one entry and refused after it. A stolen card goes on signing, and the chain draws the line where the fleet last verified it.built · emulated
What a program may doA program is held to what it declared: the disks it sees, the directory it owns, the ports it binds, the time it is given, the calls it may never make.built · emulated

The analysis

Two things the file cannot say yet.

Read against the year's losses, the floor was missing five declarations, and the first three, where the data stays, the copy and the roll call, are written (above). Each is small to write and costly to leave out; neither of the two below exists today, and both are ordered or ruled, in the order below.

The loss drillA scene that destroys a member while the fleet runs, and shows the rest serve, re-judge themselves and say what was lost. Today every scene builds a fleet and none breaks one; the emulator's version of a drone is the test this page owes.ordered · not built
A source mirrorThe kernel, the toolchain and every pinned program restored from a mirror inside the estate, so that a cloud is rebuilt with no outside network at all. Ruled already; the kernel's mirror is named as the next step of that ruling and is not done.ruled · not built

Said plainly

What this page does not claim.

  • Everything built ran in an emulator. No box of this design stands in any room yet; the first board is still parked on its hardware.
  • The floor moves one kind of data between machines: the files of a declared directory, to a declared machine, checked. What a consistent copy of a database is stays the program's to make, and nothing here restores one yet.
  • A declared reach is routing, not a filter. A box knows no way out; nothing here stops a way in, and the packet filter is a later step.
  • The intelligence is a direction on these pages. The confinement it would run under is built; the modules are not on these machines yet.
  • A small box is as resilient as its room. Its power and its link are the room's; resilience here is about how many rooms, not about one.
  • A page is not a promise. What is built is tagged, what is not is named, and the facts of the year are the newsletter's, cited in the post.
